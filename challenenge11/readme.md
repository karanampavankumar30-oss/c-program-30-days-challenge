Algorithm – 
1. Start the program.
2. Enter the water usage for 5 zones for 7 days.
3. Enter the daily water target for each zone.
4. Display the water usage details.
5. Calculate the total, average and highest water usage.
6. Check for excess water usage and sudden increases.
7. Prepare the water audit report.
8. Find and rank the zones based on excess water usage.
9. Give suggestions to save water in each zone.
10. Repeat the menu until the user selects Exit.
11. Stop the program.
